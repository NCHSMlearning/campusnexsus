CAMPUSNEXUS · WEBSITE INFORMATION

Privacy Notice

Last updated: 15 November 2026

This notice explains how CampusNexus handles information when you browse campusnexus.co.ke, submit a consultation request, use website chat or contact our team. It applies to this public website; a separate agreement or institutional privacy notice may apply to a deployed client platform.

1. Information you provide

Depending on the feature you use, we may receive your name, organisation, email address, telephone number, requested service, preferred consultation date and time, and the details you choose to include in a message. Please do not submit passwords, payment-card details, medical records or other sensitive information through general enquiry forms or chat.

2. Website and technical information

Our website and service providers may process technical information such as browser and device details, approximate usage events, timestamps, diagnostic logs and security signals. Where an analytics or consent control is presented, use it to manage the optional analytics choices offered by the site.

3. Why we use information

  • Respond to enquiries and arrange product demonstrations or consultations.
  • Assess project requirements and prepare proposals.
  • Provide requested support and maintain website functionality.
  • Protect the website, prevent abuse and troubleshoot errors.
  • Meet applicable legal and record-keeping obligations.

4. Service providers and sharing

Website infrastructure, database, communications, analytics and support features may be provided by third-party service providers acting in support of the website. Information may be processed by those providers to deliver the requested service. We do not publish enquiry details as testimonials or marketing material without an appropriate basis.

5. Retention and security

We retain information for as long as reasonably needed for the purposes described above, including follow-up, security, operational and legal requirements. We use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards, but no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Your choices and requests

You may contact us to request access to, correction of or deletion of personal information you submitted, subject to applicable law and legitimate record-keeping requirements. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

7. Children

This public website is intended for organisations and professional users, not for children to submit personal information independently.

8. Changes and contact

We may update this notice as the website and services change. The latest version will be published on this page.
